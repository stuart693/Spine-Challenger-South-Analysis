How far to trust this

This rests on one shared race, so treat the figures as a rough guide and not a forecast. A 100-mile summer race and a winter Spine race with kit, night navigation and cold are different tests, so the ratio between you may not hold. Jon's own form has also moved. How these are built

Times come from Open Tracking for Winter 2022 to 2026. Winter 2016 to 2020 have no checkpoint times there, so they are left out. The checkpoints changed in 2024, so 2022 and 2023 use 12 checkpoints and 2024 to 2026 use 8. Pooling only combines years with the same checkpoints.

Every start is taken as 08:00 on the Saturday. The Open Tracking pages list the 2024 and 2025 starts at 08:30 and the 2026 start at 10:00, so real clock times in those years were later than shown. "Finishers" counts only runners who finished. "Everyone who reached it" adds those who retired later, so early checkpoints look a little slower.

Jon's times are his actual Winter 2023 splits at the 2023 checkpoints. At the 2024 to 2026 checkpoints they are interpolated by distance along the route between his 2023 splits, so treat them as less certain, especially for Cowling and Horton. Your times are Jon's multiplied by the ratio of your Millstone 100 times (—). Place in the field assumes a normal distribution.
